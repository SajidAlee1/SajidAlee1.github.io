Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the FocusLoop mobile app ("the App"), developed by Sajid Ali ("we", "us"). It explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information you provide when you sign up:

  • Full name
  • Email address

Content you create in the App:

  • Study videos (video and audio) you record with your camera and microphone
  • Video titles, folders, and how well you rate your understanding of each video

Device information used to run the App:

  • A push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications
  • Basic technical data such as device type, operating system version and IP address, processed by our service providers when the App connects to them

The App does not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising ID, and it does not show ads.

2. Camera and microphone

The App asks for camera and microphone access only so you can record study videos. Nothing is recorded until you press record. You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account
  • To store your videos and folders so you can review them later
  • To send study reminders and notifications you have turned on
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Service providers

The App relies on these providers, which process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase – account sign-in, database and video storage (privacy policy)
  • Expo – app platform and push notification delivery (privacy policy)
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – delivers push notifications to Android devices (privacy information)

5. Data security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to your account data is restricted to your signed-in account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time:

  • In the App: Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, folders, videos and notification tokens.
  • On the web: see Delete your account, or email us.

Logging out removes your device's notification token and cancels reminders scheduled on that device.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can access and update your information in the App, and request a copy, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
