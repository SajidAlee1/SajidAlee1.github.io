Delete your account

Remove your FocusLoop account and all of its data.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name and email)
  • All your folders
  • All your recorded videos and their files
  • Your devices' notification tokens

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We don't keep a copy of deleted data.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

Open FocusLoop → Profile → Account → Delete account, then confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: request it online

If you can't use the app, send a request with the email address of your account. We process requests within 30 days.
