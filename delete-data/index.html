Delete your data

Remove videos, folders or reminders and keep your account.

Delete a video (in the app)

Open FocusLoop → Files → open a folder → tap ⋯ next to the video → Delete video, then confirm.

The video and its recorded file are deleted immediately and permanently.

Delete a folder (in the app)

Open FocusLoop → Files → tap ⋯ next to the folder → Delete folder, then confirm.

A folder must be empty first, so videos are never removed by accident. Delete or move its videos, then delete the folder.

Stop notifications and remove your device token

Turn off Profile → Study reminders, or log out. Logging out deletes this device's notification token from our servers and cancels its scheduled reminders.

Ask us to delete specific data

If you can't use the app, email the address of your account and tell us what to delete. We process requests within 30 days.

Email a data deletion request

What is kept

Your account (name and email) and anything you didn't delete stay until you delete them or delete your account. We don't keep copies of deleted videos.
